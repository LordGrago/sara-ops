# Brand Context — Sara Vinitz

**Sara Vinitz** is a культуролог (cultural scholar) and art guide. Not a commercial art consultant — a worldview transmitter.

## Core thesis

Art consulting as tension relief leading to transformation. Client moves from *"I don't understand this world"* to *"I move freely in it."*

**Key quote:** *"Красота — это форма сознания."* (Beauty is a form of consciousness.)

## Client archetypes

1. **Entrepreneurs** — first art purchases (fear of mistakes, need guidance)
2. **Families** — building legacy collections (meaning, heritage, generational thinking)
3. **Corporate** — art as strategic tool (not decoration — consciousness)

## UTP pillars

1. **Access** — top tier of art world is 1–2 calls away (Hermitage, Pushkin, Garage, GES-2)
2. **Independence** — zero financial ties to galleries/artists. Can say "I don't believe in X"
3. **Navigation without snobbism** — code decryption in human language, dignity for non-experts

## Voice

Confident but not arrogant. Warm but not soft. Direct but not blunt. Cultured but not elitist. Speaks like a brilliant friend over wine, not a professor in a lecture.

## Visual system (90/10 rule)

90% institutional white-space clarity + 10% ornamental "incrustation" from Sara's cultural DNA: Russian folk, Jewish decorative tradition, Azerbaijani textile, LA kitsch/pop. Brand colors: Lime `#DAEF5A`, Lavender `#B98EC4`, Hot Pink `#F0156B`.

## Content pillars

- **Decoding** (навигация) — explaining art, movements, market
- **Inner World** (глубина) — personal reflections, studio visits
- **Provocation** (честность) — challenging conventions, debunking myths
- **Collection Stories** (трансформация) — how art changes lives

## Vocabulary

**Use:** Ясность, Проводник, Расшифровка, Контекст, Вкус, Навигация, Осознанный выбор, Без страха, Честно, Свобода

**Avoid:** Элитарный, Эксклюзивный (as gatekeeping), Инвестиция (as primary frame), Обучение (implies hierarchy), Должны, Тренд, Модный

## Anti-positioning

Sara is NOT: Queen/Diva, academic snob, institutional curator, info-blogger, dealer, or decorator.
